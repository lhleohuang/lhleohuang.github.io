Lianghuan Huang

I'm AI researcher at Mila - Quebec AI Institute, advised by Prof. Shahab Bakhtiari. Previously, I graduated with a physics major from the University of Pennsylvania, working with Profs. Konrad Kording and Osbert Bastani.

My research interests lie in self-supervised learning and world models. I’m interested in how AI systems learn representations that capture the underlying structure and dynamics of their environment, and how learning objectives, architectures, and data selection shape the information these representations preserve. I explore these questions through theoretical analysis and controlled experiments, often taking inspirations from neuroscience, with the ultimate goal of learning representations that support action, planning, and generalization.

Past Projects

Formalizing the Binding Problem

How do Vision Transformers understand scenes with multiple objects without confusing their features? We formalized feature-to-object "binding" with an information theoretic approach, and devised a probing method to quantify binding information in any artificial model representations. Our work has important implications for object-centric perception in visual reasoning and embodied AI. Published at ICML 2026.
